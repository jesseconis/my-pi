The voice extension receives assistant events from Pi, extracts the configured content, optionally summarizes it, and sends the final text to the local Kokoro server. For regular prose, the fastest path is direct synthesis: the server receives plain text, generates a WAV file, and the extension plays it. This benchmark text is intentionally longer than a status message but shorter than a full assistant response, so it can reveal whether inference startup, transfer overhead, or per-character synthesis dominates total latency.
